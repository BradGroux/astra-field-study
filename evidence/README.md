# Reviewed observational evidence

Evidence packages use neutral study identifiers rather than contributor names. These are reviewed aggregates, separate from the participant submissions in [`submissions/`](../submissions/README.md).

| Study | Evidence version | Description |
|---|---|---|
| [Study 0001](study-0001-v1/README.md) | 1 | Six days of GPT-6 Astra usage and corrective steering. |

Use `study-NNNN-vV` for future packages. A study ID identifies a package, not a unique participant. Follow the [format and naming rules](../docs/observational-evidence-format.md#neutral-study-identifiers) and the [privacy requirements](../docs/privacy.md).

Neutral names reduce direct identification in paths and labels. They do not make public evidence anonymous: Git authorship, prior history, public source references, and linked publications remain observable. Do not submit private identity mappings or raw history. Required license notices and proper public-source attribution remain intact.
